## 2. Inadmissible Claims (Strictly Prohibited)

The following claims are **empirically unsupported** and are explicitly excluded from the manuscript:
1. *Universal Superiority*: Claiming Approach B is universally superior to Approach A across all workloads (refuted by Workload D at $r \le 4$ and Workload C at $r \le 2$).
2. *Causal Compaction Throughput Scaling*: Claiming high-concurrency speedups in Workload C prove compaction concurrency benefits (refuted by <1.6% exposure duration and inconclusive CIs).
3. *Zero-Overhead Abstraction*: Claiming snapshot pinning has zero computational overhead (refuted by descriptor allocation and retirement overhead in Workload D).
4. *Identical Operation Sequences*: Claiming Approach A and B replay identical operations (refuted by mode index $mIdx$ in seed formula; workloads are statistically equivalent but PRNG streams diverge).
5. *Cross-Platform Generalization*: Claiming ext4 results generalize directly to Windows NTFS or bare-metal Linux.
